Google Business Profile (GBP) Setup & Optimization — SK Web Solutions

Purpose
- Help SK Web Solutions appear in local searches for Hyderabad (Maps + Local Pack).
- Provide step-by-step setup, best practices, copy templates, and review request examples.

Before you start
- You'll need a Google account (preferably a company account, e.g., skwebdeveloper@proton.me).
- Business address or service-area details (if remote work only, set service area and hide street address).
- High-quality images: office exterior, interior, team, projects, logo (800×800+), and cover image (1024×576+).

Step 1 — Create the GBP
1. Visit https://business.google.com/ and sign in with the company Google account.
2. Click "Add your business" → Enter business name: "SK Web Solutions".
3. Select category: primary: "Web Designer" or "Web Design Agency" (choose the closest match). Add secondary categories: "Web Developer", "E-commerce Service".
4. Enter service area: Hyderabad (and other cities you serve). If you have an office address, add it; otherwise choose "No" and specify service area.
5. Add phone number: +91 7095244790 and website: https://<your-domain> (update to actual domain).
6. Verify ownership: request postcard, phone, or email verification (follow Google prompts).

Step 2 — Complete the profile (high impact)
- Business description (use local keywords naturally):
  "SK Web Solutions is a Hyderabad-based web development agency delivering responsive websites, e-commerce stores, and local SEO services for small and medium businesses in Hyderabad."
- Business hours: add accurate hours and mark special hours for holidays.
- Services: add services with short descriptions and prices where applicable (Web Design, E-commerce, SEO, Landing Pages).
- Add attributes: "Online appointments" (if using Calendly), "Accepts online orders" (only if relevant), wheelchair accessibility (if you have a physical office).

Step 3 — Add images & videos (SEO + trust)
- Upload logo (square 512×512 or higher), cover photo, team photos, and project screenshots.
- Add short video (15–30s) showcasing a project or office (helps ranking and conversions).
- Tag images in the CMS or site with descriptive filenames (e.g., project-hyderabad-realestate-before.jpg).

Step 4 — Get reviews & respond
- Ask clients for Google reviews shortly after project delivery.
- Provide a one-click review link: In GBP dashboard → Home → Get more reviews → Share review form link.
- Suggested review request message (WhatsApp / email):
  "Hi [Client Name], thanks for working with SK Web Solutions. If you’re happy with the website, could you please leave a quick Google review? It helps small businesses a lot. Here’s the link: [GBP review link] — Thank you! — Sarang Kumar"
- Respond to all reviews (positive and negative) promptly and professionally.

Step 5 — Use Posts and Services
- Publish GBP Posts weekly/monthly: Project launches, blog post links (e.g., 'Top 10 Website Design Trends in 2026'), offers, or events.
- Add Services entries (these appear on your profile): short title, description, and price range.

Step 6 — Track & optimize
- Check GBP Insights: views, searches, customer actions, and directions requests.
- Compare performance after adding photos, posts, and review activity (improvements often follow within weeks).

Technical & Schema suggestions
- Ensure site has LocalBusiness JSON-LD on homepage with accurate address, phone, openingHours, and sameAs links (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram).
- Add Service Schema for major service pages (already added to some pages).
- Add Review Schema where appropriate for case studies (only if you can display real reviews).
